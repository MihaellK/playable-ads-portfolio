AI Workflow Integration

I structured my workflow by integrating LLMs as true Tech Leads and software architects. I configured custom AIs with strict engineering guidelines, acting as a scale multiplier that allows me to exercise strong ownership and operate projects independently with the speed and technical rigor of an entire team.

The efficiency of this methodology is proven in both rapid prototyping and extreme-pressure deliveries. An empirical case was the construction of this very portfolio: in a sprint of just 4 hours, I structured, developed, and packaged three distinct playable ads (simple, yet containing the complete funnel and pillars of a playable ad) using Phaser 3, PixiJS, and Three.js. I delegated to the AI the generation of boilerplates, the Vite configuration for single-file HTML packaging (MRAID standards), and the assembly of data-driven architectures (like gameConfig.ts), which easily allows for A/B testing without code duplication. By automating this initial load, I could dedicate my critical judgment and engineering time exclusively to polishing the game juice, optimizing retention in the first 3 seconds, and ensuring that themes and mechanics could be adapted efficiently without breaking the game code.

Furthermore, during the management of my indie studio, I sustained the solo development of a project that secured a R$ 50,000 investment from Sampa Games and the delivery of a stable build for exhibition at Gamescom Latam. In these intense cycles, I instruct the AI with guardrails focused on mobile and browser constraints: it acts as a real-time defensive code auditor, early diagnosing memory leaks, Garbage Collection spikes, and unnecessary allocations within physics and rendering loops. Additionally, I integrate generative tools to create and adapt basic visual effects, shortening the asset pipeline.

For me, AI-first development does not replace critical game design or manual optimization. On the contrary, it absorbs the grunt work and ensures code stability, giving me the autonomy to take an advertising brief and turn it into an interactive, responsive, and polished experience. This is how I balance technical quality, execution speed, and scope control.
